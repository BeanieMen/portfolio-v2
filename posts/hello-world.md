After way too much procrastination, I finally built a new website and added a blog section.

## What's this about?

I wanted a place to write about the things I'm building, problems I've solved, and thoughts on infrastructure, music, and software in general. Not everything needs to be a polished article — sometimes a short post is enough.

## The Stack

The portfolio is built with **Next.js 15** (App Router), **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS v4**. The animated grid background tracks your cursor and renders a purple glow — pure CSS and `requestAnimationFrame`, no libraries.

- Music production
